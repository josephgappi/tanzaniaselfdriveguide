Plan your fuel before the remote sections

Fuel planning is an important part of a Tanzania self-drive safari. Some parts of the northern safari circuit have long stretches where you should not expect to find another convenient place to refuel. The safest approach is to fill your tank before entering these remote sections and plan your next reliable fuel stop in advance.

Quick answer

For many northern Tanzania self-drive routes, start with a full tank in Arusha. After Tarangire, plan your next major refuelling around Karatu if you are continuing toward Ngorongoro and Serengeti. In Serengeti, the Seronera area is an important fuel point. Routes toward Lake Natron require additional planning around Mto wa Mbu, Wasso and Longido, depending on the direction of travel.

1. Start with a full tank in Arusha

Arusha is the main preparation point for many northern Tanzania self-drive safaris. Before leaving the city, fill the vehicle's fuel tank and make sure you understand the vehicle's expected range.

This is particularly important when your next destination is Tarangire National Park and you are planning to continue deeper into the safari circuit afterwards.

Recommended approach

Start the safari with a full tank in Arusha rather than beginning the remote part of the journey with a partially filled tank.

2. Arusha → Tarangire National Park

If your route starts in Arusha and continues to Tarangire National Park, filling the tank in Arusha should give you the fuel needed for the Tarangire section, depending on the vehicle, driving conditions and how much driving you do inside the park.

The important point is what happens after Tarangire. Do not simply continue into the next remote section without considering where your next fuel opportunity will be.

Arusha — Fill tank → Tarangire → Karatu — Refill

3. Fill up in Karatu before Ngorongoro

If you are travelling from Tarangire toward Ngorongoro and Serengeti, Karatu is a key fuel-planning point.

Fill the tank in Karatu before entering the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. From this point onward, you should plan carefully because you are entering a much more remote part of the northern safari circuit.

Important fuel stop

Karatu should be treated as a major refuelling opportunity before continuing toward Ngorongoro and Serengeti.

Once you have filled in Karatu, plan your remaining fuel around your route, vehicle consumption and the amount of driving you expect to do inside the conservation and park areas.

4. Karatu → Ngorongoro → Serengeti

After filling in Karatu, many self-drivers continue through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area toward Serengeti.

The important principle is simple: do not plan on finding another normal town-style fuel station immediately after leaving Karatu. Enter this section with the fuel you need for the journey.

Karatu — Fill tank → Ngorongoro → Serengeti → Seronera — Refill

5. Refuelling in the Seronera area of Serengeti

Once you reach Serengeti, the Seronera area becomes an important point in your fuel planning.

If you are spending several days driving around Serengeti, remember that safari driving can involve considerably more distance than simply travelling from one accommodation location to another. Game drives, route changes and exploring different areas can all increase fuel consumption.

Do not calculate fuel only from road distance

Your fuel requirement depends not only on the distance between towns but also on the amount of driving you do inside national parks and remote areas.

6. If you travel from Serengeti toward Lake Natron

A Serengeti to Lake Natron route requires particularly careful fuel planning because you are moving through remote areas.

After using the Seronera fuel opportunity, plan the next important refuelling around Wasso before continuing farther toward the Lake Natron and Longido side of the route.

Seronera — Fill → Wasso — Fill → Longido — Fill → Arusha

Wasso is an important planning point on this route. Once you have passed Wasso, Longido provides another opportunity to refuel before continuing toward Arusha.

7. Wasso → Longido → Arusha

If your journey continues from the Lake Natron side toward Arusha, plan to use the fuel opportunity at Wasso and then Longido before returning to Arusha.

This route demonstrates why fuel planning should be based on the complete itinerary rather than simply asking whether a vehicle has enough fuel for the next day's drive.

8. If you go from Tarangire toward Lake Natron

There is another important fuel strategy if Lake Natron is part of your itinerary immediately after Tarangire.

In this case, plan to fill up at Mto wa Mbu before continuing toward the Lake Natron route.

Arusha — Fill → Tarangire → Mto wa Mbu — Fill → Wasso — Fill → Serengeti

After Wasso, continue according to your itinerary and refuel again around Seronera when entering or travelling through Serengeti.

9. Returning from Serengeti toward Arusha

If you are driving back toward Arusha from Serengeti, plan your return fuel rather than assuming that the fuel remaining in the tank will be enough for the entire journey.

After using the Seronera fuel opportunity, Karatu becomes an important refuelling point when you are travelling back toward Arusha.

Seronera → Karatu — Refill → Arusha

11. Do not plan fuel by kilometres alone

A self-drive safari is different from a normal road trip. You may spend hours driving slowly through a national park, stop frequently for wildlife, take alternative tracks or make additional excursions that were not part of your original road-distance calculation.

Your vehicle also matters. A fully equipped 4x4 carrying passengers, camping equipment and luggage may consume fuel differently from a lightly loaded road vehicle.

For this reason, always leave yourself a sensible reserve rather than planning to arrive at the next fuel point with the fuel gauge almost empty.

12. Fuel checklist before entering a remote area

Fill the tank before leaving a major fuel point.
Know your vehicle's approximate fuel range.
Consider the amount of driving you will do inside parks.
Do not rely on the fuel gauge reaching nearly empty.
Check your planned route before leaving.
Ask about the vehicle's fuel consumption before departure.
Consider remote sections when calculating your fuel reserve.
Refill whenever a planned reliable fuel opportunity is available.

13. Fuel planning works together with your route plan

Fuel is only one part of preparing for a Tanzania self-drive safari. Before leaving Arusha, also check your route, road conditions, camping arrangements, park rules and the capabilities of your 4x4.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I fill fuel before starting a Tanzania self-drive safari?

Arusha is an important starting point for northern Tanzania self-drive trips. Starting with a full tank gives you a better margin before reaching the next planned fuel point.

Where should I refuel after Tarangire?

If you are continuing toward Ngorongoro and Serengeti, Karatu is an important place to refuel before entering the Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

Where can I refuel in Serengeti?

The Seronera area is an important fuel point for travellers in Serengeti. Fuel planning should take into account the amount of driving you expect to do inside the park.

Where should I refuel when travelling toward Lake Natron?

Depending on your route, Mto wa Mbu, Wasso and Longido are important fuel-planning points. The exact sequence depends on whether you travel toward Natron from Tarangire or from the Serengeti side.

Should I wait until the fuel gauge is low before refuelling?

No. On remote safari routes, it is better to use planned fuel opportunities and maintain a sensible reserve rather than waiting until the tank is nearly empty.
