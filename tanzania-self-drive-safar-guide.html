Planning to drive Tanzania yourself?

Self-driving through Tanzania is very different from taking a conventional guided safari. Understanding the roads, national parks, vehicle requirements, camping, costs and local rules can make a major difference to your trip.

This guide is built to answer the practical questions travellers ask before getting behind the wheel.


We focus on useful information rather than simply telling you where to book a safari.
