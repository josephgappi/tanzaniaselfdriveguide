Frequently Asked Questions

Using the Tanzania self-drive resources

A few simple questions about where to begin.

What is the best place to start planning a Tanzania self-drive trip?

Start with the Start Here guide. Then move through driving, routes, national parks and costs before finishing with the self-drive checklist.

Do I need to read every resource?

No. Use the resources according to your trip. Someone who is already familiar with Tanzania may want to go directly to routes and parks, while a first-time self-driver should begin with the general planning and driving guides.

Where should I check official information?

Use the relevant Tanzanian authority for official rules, notices, park information and requirements. The official-source links on this page provide useful starting points.

Is this website a safari booking site?

Tanzania Self Drive Guide is structured as an independent information resource. Travelers who need vehicle or practical safari support can separately consider an experienced Tanzania self-drive specialist.

When should I use the self-drive checklist?

Use it during the final preparation stage and again shortly before leaving, after your route, vehicle and main trip arrangements have been decided.
