1. Introduction

Welcome to Tanzania Self Drive Guide. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you visit our website.

Tanzania Self Drive Guide is an independent informational resource created to help travelers plan self-drive journeys in Tanzania.

The website provides practical information about driving, roads, national parks, routes, camping, vehicle rental, trip preparation and other aspects of independent travel in Tanzania.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used and what happens when you follow links to external websites.

Important: Tanzania Self Drive Guide is an independent information resource. It is not a government website and is not an official website of TANAPA, NCAA or another Tanzanian government authority.

2. Information We Collect

You can browse most of Tanzania Self Drive Guide without directly providing personal information.

Depending on how you use the website, information may include:

  • Your name, email address or other contact information if you voluntarily provide it.
  • Information contained in a message or enquiry you send us.
  • Technical information that may be provided automatically by your browser or hosting environment.
  • Basic information about how the website is accessed, where analytics or similar tools are enabled.

We do not ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information simply to browse the website.

3. How We Use Information

Where information is voluntarily provided to us, it may be used for purposes including:

  • Responding to questions or enquiries.
  • Providing information requested by a visitor.
  • Communicating about a website-related enquiry.
  • Improving the website and its content.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Understanding general website performance where analytics tools are enabled.

We do not intend to use information for unrelated purposes simply because it has been provided to us.

4. Contact Forms, Email and Enquiries

If you contact us through an email address, contact form, WhatsApp link or another communication method displayed on this website, the information you provide may be used to respond to your enquiry.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through ordinary website forms or messaging channels unless it is specifically necessary.

Some pages on this website may direct visitors to external service providers for vehicle rental, accommodation, travel services or other arrangements.

Once you communicate directly with an external provider, that provider's own privacy policy and terms will apply.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. They can be used to remember preferences, support website functionality, improve security or understand website usage.

Tanzania Self Drive Guide may use cookies or similar technologies where they are necessary for website functionality, security, analytics or other legitimate website purposes.

If third-party analytics, advertising or other services are added to the website, those services may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

You can normally control or remove cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services to provide or support functions such as hosting, security, analytics, fonts, communication, maps or other website features.

Where third-party services are used, those providers may process certain technical or personal information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy information provided by third-party services before submitting personal information directly to them.

7. Links to External Websites

Tanzania Self Drive Guide contains links to external websites. These may include official authorities, national park organisations, vehicle rental companies, accommodation providers, supermarkets, fuel stations, travel businesses and other useful resources.

We provide these links as resources for travelers. However, we do not control the privacy practices or security of external websites.

When you leave Tanzania Self Drive Guide and visit another website, that website's own privacy policy, terms and practices apply.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information that is submitted through the website and to maintain appropriate website security.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Visitors should therefore understand that information transmitted over the internet may carry some level of risk.

9. How Long We Keep Information

If you contact us directly, information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate interests.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or securely disposed of where reasonably practical.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your circumstances and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information that we hold about you.

These may include asking how your information is handled, requesting correction of inaccurate information or requesting deletion where there is no legitimate reason for retaining it.

If you have a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended primarily for people planning travel and self-drive journeys in Tanzania.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information through the website, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology, services or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed.

13. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy?

If you have a question about privacy or how information is handled on Tanzania Self Drive Guide, you can contact us.
